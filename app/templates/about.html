ABOUT

I am Key Castro, a developer focused on real estate business systems.

I build web applications around the records, decisions, handoffs, and day-to-day work that property and real-estate teams need to manage clearly.

HOW I APPROACH A BUILD

Understand the operation before designing the system.

The goal is not to add software for its own sake. The system should make the real workflow easier to understand, follow, and maintain.

  1. 01
    Understand

    Map the people, records, handoffs, delays, and decisions that shape the work.

  2. 02
    Build

    Shape the screens, database, permissions, and actions around the agreed process.

  3. 03
    Make it usable

    Keep navigation, status, testing, documentation, and handoff understandable.

Real estate and property operations.
