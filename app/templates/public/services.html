COMMON QUESTIONS

Simple answers.

Does RSF sell ready-made systems?

No. The systems shown on this website are examples of work RSF has built. Client systems are built around each business’s needs.

Can I take care of the system myself after delivery?

Yes. There is no RSF support fee if you choose to handle hosting, updates, backups, and fixes yourself.

Can RSF support the system after delivery?

Yes. Paid RSF support is optional and priced by agreement.

Can I ask for changes later?

Yes. Small changes can be part of paid RSF support. Big changes are priced separately.
