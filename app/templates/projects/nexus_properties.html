01 · PROBLEM & SOLUTION

Keep private property inventory searchable, current, and clearly owned.

BUSINESS PROBLEMOff-market stock loses value when agents cannot quickly find it or trust that it is still available.

The workflow needs clear ownership, useful search, current status, and a way to prevent stale records from remaining active indefinitely.

BUILT SOLUTIONOne internal web application for the listing lifecycle.

The completed system combines private access, structured listings, marketplace search, ownership controls, reconfirmation, automatic expiry, history, and management settings.
